CarbonWatch Kenya

Transparent carbon credit flows and community voice for rangeland and forest carbon projects in Kenya.

National framework for Kenya’s conservancies

Project focus

CarbonWatch is a national framework for KWCA (Kenya Wildlife Conservancies Association): public-good technical infrastructure for the entire country. We track Verra-registered projects across Kenya—including the Northern Kenya Rangeland Project (NRT), Komaza, Boomitra, and KCSA—and connect communities through communication tools, social measurement (SCOUT), and a front-end dashboard for data description and analysis.

Our work is piloted at the scale of 230+ conservancies and 110 million hectares of land under KWCA—one of our field sites, and one of many potential applications. The system is built for long-term incubation and scaling beyond carbon projects: governance, livelihoods, and conservancy monitoring at national scale.

Data flow

SMS/IVR and manual entries feed into a single central repository; the Public Dashboard is the front-end interface for data description and analysis. Community feedback flows via SMS/IVR; the SCOUT tool maps inputs to empowerment dimensions (ILRI WELI). The dashboard shows Kenya project-level data and per-conservancy figures where disclosed.
